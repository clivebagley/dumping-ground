Shepshed by-election

Labour candidate: Jane Lennie

Your Labour candidate

Jane Lennie was born and brought up in Shepshed and is the Labour councillor for Shepshed West on Charnwood Borough Council. She is standing in the Shepshed division of Leicestershire County Council.
